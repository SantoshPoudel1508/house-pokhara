Brief for Soil Test / Geotechnical Engineer

Show this to the company that will test the soil before construction starts

Dear Engineer, we are planning to build a G+2 family house (foundation designed for G+3) on a 30×60 ft plot in Pokhara. Below are our specific requirements for the soil investigation.

What We Require You to Do
  • Minimum 2 boreholes on our plot — one near the front, one near the rear
  • Drilling depth: minimum 25 feet (8 metres), or as required by soil conditions
  • SPT (Standard Penetration Test) at every 1.5m of depth
  • Groundwater table level determination
  • Soil classification at each depth (clay, sand, gravel, rock)
  • We will be physically present during all drilling work — please inform us in advance
  • We will take photographs and video of the drilling process
Report Requirements
  • Printed report on company letterhead, signed by a licensed engineer
  • Our specific plot address must appear on the report
  • SBC (Safe Bearing Capacity) value clearly stated in kN/m²
  • Borehole log with depth measurements and soil samples noted
  • Groundwater level
  • Recommended foundation type for G+3-capable design
  • Seismic site classification
  • Settlement risk assessment
  • Earthquake zone design recommendations
⚠️ We Will Not Accept: A report without our specific plot address. A report delivered within 24 hours of a 2-borehole test (not enough time). A report without clear SBC value. A generic report that appears copied from another site.

Brief for Structural Engineer

The most important professional for earthquake safety

Our House Details
Plot30 × 60 feet, rectangular, North-East facing
Construction PlanG+2 (Ground floor + 2 upper floors)
Foundation RequirementMust be designed for G+3 (even though we are building G+2 now)
LocationPokhara, Nepal — Seismic Zone V (high earthquake risk)
Wall MaterialAAC Blocks preferred. Red brick acceptable.
Our Requirements From You
  • Full structural design based on the soil test report we will provide
  • Design must comply with Nepal National Building Code (NBC) for seismic zone
  • Column layout must be symmetric for earthquake performance
  • Concrete grade: minimum M25 for all columns and beams; M20 for slabs
  • Steel grade: Fe 500D TMT bars throughout
  • All columns must have ductile detailing (earthquake-resistant reinforcement)
  • Plinth beam at ground level around entire perimeter
  • Lintel beam above every door and window opening
  • Complete reinforcement bar-bending schedule (drawing showing exactly how steel is bent)
  • Shuttering load calculations included
  • Minimum 6 site visits during construction (foundation, column steel check, slab steel check, post-construction)
  • You must check steel placement BEFORE each concrete pour — no pour without your approval
⚠️ We Will Not Accept: Design without soil test data. Generic column sizes "estimated." Design for G+2 only (must be G+3 capable). No site visits after handing over drawings. Any change in concrete or steel grade without written justification.

Brief for Architect

Our design requirements and the feel we want for our family home

House Character and Style

We want a "Modern Warm Minimal Family House." This means: clean and calm look, warm materials (wood tones, earthy colours), good natural light, no overly trendy or Instagram-style design. The house should feel peaceful, premium, and timeless — not flashy.

Floor Planning Requirements
FloorWhat Must Be on This FloorSpecial Notes
Ground FloorParents' bedroom (ground floor only — for old age access). Kitchen. Living/dining. Guest toilet. Puja room. Utility room. Shoe entrance area (mudroom at main entrance).Must be elderly-friendly: wide doors (min 1000mm), no level differences inside, anti-skid floors throughout, grab bar provision in one bathroom.
First FloorOur master bedroom (with ensuite). 2 children's/guest rooms. Family lounge. Home office/study. Common bathroom.Master bedroom must get morning sunlight (NE advantage). Study can later become music/hobby room — plan thick walls for that room.
Second Floor2-bedroom independent flat layout (bedroom, hall, kitchen, bathroom).Must be able to function independently for future rental. Separate staircase access and separate meter provision.
TerraceAccessible terrace space. Provision for solar water heater and solar panels.Slope to drain on terrace slab. Light-coloured tiles.
Design Requirements We Specifically Need
  • Cross ventilation: Every room must have openings on at least two walls for through-air flow
  • Service zones: All bathrooms on every floor must be stacked directly above each other (same position on each floor). Kitchen also aligned vertically. This groups all plumbing in one area.
  • Entrance mudroom: A covered outdoor porch (minimum 1.2m × 1.8m) with built-in bench and shoe cabinet before the main entrance door
  • No sharp edges: All wall corners to use bullnose corner beading — rounded, not 90-degree sharp corners. This applies throughout the entire house.
  • Staircase: Straight or L-shaped. Minimum 1200mm wide. Riser maximum 160mm. Under-staircase usable storage room.
  • Setbacks: Front: 8–10 feet. Sides: 3–4 feet each. Rear: 4–6 feet (sewage side — important for waterproofing)
  • Music/hobby room: One room on first floor to be designated as future music/hobby room — use thicker walls (230mm) and plan extra electrical conduit in ceiling and walls
  • Storage: Every floor must have dedicated storage/utility space. Under-staircase room. Dust-proof store room with sealed door.
  • Rear garden: Leave 4–6 feet rear setback for a vegetable garden area. One outdoor water tap provision at rear.
  • Window sizes: Living room windows minimum 1500mm × 1350mm. Bedroom windows minimum 1200mm × 1200mm. Bathroom windows frosted glass, high on wall.
  • Window sill design: All window sills (external ledge) in granite or Kota stone, sloped outward for rain drainage, with waterproof sealant at wall junction.
⚠️ We Will Not Accept: Rooms with windows on only one wall. Bathrooms on different positions on different floors (must be stacked). Design without mudroom/entrance shoe area. Sharp 90-degree wall corners throughout (bullnose is mandatory). Maximizing build area at the cost of setbacks — we need the breathing space.

Process Requirements — Must Be Followed
  • Structural engineer approval before every concrete pour: No concrete poured without structural engineer's sign-off that steel is correct
  • Curing mandatory: Every structural element (footing, column, beam, slab) must be water-cured for minimum 7 days. Wet burlap or ponding — not just wet for one day.
  • Vibrator machine use: Concrete vibrator machine must be used during every structural concrete pour. Not optional.
  • Cover blocks: Plastic or concrete cover blocks must be placed on all steel before concrete pour to maintain correct cover distance
  • No excavation or pouring during active rain
  • All wall corners: Bullnose corner bead on EVERY internal wall corner — no sharp 90-degree corners. This applies throughout the entire house including staircase area.
  • Window sill: All external window sills in granite or Kota stone, laid with waterproof mortar, sloped outward, with silicone sealant at wall junction
  • DPC: Damp proof course (waterproof concrete layer) at plinth level before walls start
  • Anti-termite treatment: Applied around foundation before backfilling soil
Payment Terms (Non-Negotiable)
  • Maximum 20% advance before work begins
  • All remaining payments by milestone only — we will inspect before releasing payment
  • Final 10% held back for 3 months after completion to cover any defects found
  • If any material is substituted without our written approval, the cost of correcting it is the contractor's responsibility
⚠️ Immediate Disqualification: Any contractor who says the soil test is not needed. Any contractor who refuses to put materials and grades in writing. Any contractor who demands more than 30% advance. Any contractor who says structural engineer visits are unnecessary.

Brief for Plumber

All water supply, drainage, hot water, and special system requirements

Before Starting — Must Be Done First
  • Provide a complete plumbing layout drawing showing every pipe route, every tap point, every drain point — BEFORE any pipe is laid or any wall is opened
  • We will review and approve this drawing before work begins
Pipe Materials Required
UseRequired Pipe MaterialNot Acceptable
Cold water supply inside houseCPVC pipe, branded (Ashirvad, Supreme, or equivalent)Unknown local brand pipe
Hot water supply linesCPVC pipe only (must be marked CPVC — safe for hot water)Regular uPVC or PVC for hot water lines — will warp and fail
All drain pipes inside houseSWR (Soil Waste Rain) PVC, heavy gauge, brandedThin walled regular PVC
Underground main drainHDPE pipe (black, heavy duty)Thin PVC underground
Design Rules We Require
  • Service zones: All bathrooms on each floor must be directly above each other (same position on every floor). Kitchen also aligned vertically. This is not optional.
  • P-trap at every drain point: Every floor drain, every wash basin drain, every kitchen sink drain must have a P-trap (water seal U-bend) fitted. No exceptions.
  • Stopcock valve at every room branch: Every bathroom and kitchen must have its own shut-off valve so water can be stopped to just that room without stopping the whole house
  • Isolation valve under every toilet
  • Drain slope: All drain pipes must slope at minimum 1:40 gradient (2.5 cm drop per metre). We will test water flow before walls are closed.
  • Hot water pipe insulation: All hot water supply pipes wrapped with foam pipe lagging
  • Solar water heater pipe provision: Run cold and hot water pipes to roof terrace for future solar water heater connection (even if not installing now)
  • AC condensate drain: Each AC unit location must have a dedicated drain pipe to carry condensation water to drain — not left to drip down wall
  • Underground sump tank location: Rear of house (as per architect's drawing)
  • Washing machine drain standpipe: Minimum 60 cm height before drain connection
Testing Before Closing Walls
  • All supply pipes must be pressure-tested at 3 bar for 30 minutes with no drop — before plastering
  • All drain pipes must be flow-tested with water — water must flow freely to drain before plastering
  • Water flow test after bathroom tiles laid — pour water anywhere on floor, must flow to drain within 10 seconds
⚠️ We Will Check: All pipe packaging/brand before installation begins. P-trap under every drain before tiles are laid. Drain flow before plastering. We will reject work that does not meet these standards and payment will be withheld until corrected.

Brief for Electrician

Complete electrical requirements including master switches, separate meter, and all appliances

Before Starting — Drawing Required
  • Provide a complete room-by-room electrical layout drawing showing every circuit, every socket location, every switch location — BEFORE any wire is laid
  • This drawing must be approved by us before work begins
Special Electrical Features We Require
  • Per-room master switch: A single switch OUTSIDE every room door that switches off all lights inside that room when pressed. This requires a relay or master control switch module per room.
  • Whole-house master switch: One main switch (at main entrance or panel) that switches off all lights and fans in the entire house. This switch must NOT affect: refrigerator, CCTV cameras, internet router, security system, inverter output. These must be on separate "always-on" circuits that bypass the master switch.
  • Separate meter for second floor (G+2): The entire second floor must have a completely separate electricity meter and separate distribution board from day one. All second floor circuits must run from this separate panel — not from the ground/first floor panel.

Safety Requirements — Mandatory
  • ELCB (Earth Leakage Circuit Breaker) on every bathroom circuit, kitchen circuit, all outdoor circuits, and washing machine circuit
  • All metal switch boxes and socket boxes must be earthed
  • All wiring inside rigid PVC conduit — no direct laying in plaster
  • All wire joins inside covered junction boxes — no open joins behind walls
  • MCB brands: ABB, Schneider, or Legrand only — no local/unbranded MCBs
  • Wire brand: ISI-marked copper wire from reputed brand (Finolex, Polycab, or equivalent)
  • Earthing resistance test with digital Earth Resistance Meter after completion — must read below 5 ohms. Test to be done in our presence.
⚠️ We Will Check: Wire brand printed on spool jacket each day. MCB brand. Earthing test result in our presence. We will reject any circuit without ELCB where required. Geyser switches must be OUTSIDE bathrooms (safety requirement).

Brief for Waterproofing Contractor

Terrace, bathrooms, foundation, and rear wall requirements

Terrace / Roof Waterproofing
  • Product brand: Show us the sealed tin/container before opening. We will verify brand. Acceptable: Dr. Fixit, Fosroc, or equivalent certified product.
  • Number of coats: Minimum 2 coats of polymer membrane. First coat allowed to fully cure before second coat. No shortcuts.
  • Application area: Terrace slab + all parapet walls up to top + 300mm onto parapet wall outer face
  • Waterproof screed first (PCC with waterproofing admixture), then membrane coats
  • Flood test mandatory: After membrane is complete, create a temporary water dam and fill terrace to 50mm depth. Leave for minimum 48 hours. We will inspect the ceiling of the room below for any dampness. No payment released until flood test is passed and signed off.
  • Protection tiles to be laid AFTER flood test is passed
  • Light-coloured terrace tiles (white or light grey) to reflect heat
Bathroom Waterproofing
  • 2 coats of waterproof membrane on all bathroom floors and walls up to 600mm (2 feet) height
  • In shower area: walls waterproofed to full shower height
  • 24-hour water retention test before bathroom tile laying begins
Other Areas
  • Rear wall (sewage-side): Extra waterproofing treatment + proper drainage slope away from house. Anti-damp backfill material.
  • All window and door frames: Waterproof sealant bead at junction of frame and wall — inside and outside
  • DPC at plinth level — already done by contractor, you to verify and confirm continuity
⚠️ We Will Check: Product brand before opening. We will count empty containers against area treated to verify quantity used. Flood test will be done with us present before any terrace tile is laid.

Brief for Tile Layer / Mason

Flooring, anti-skid, slope, no sharp edges

Our Requirements
  • Batch number check before starting: Before laying any tiles in any room, check that all tile boxes have the same batch number. Do not mix batches — there will be colour variation.
  • Anti-skid rating for bathrooms: Bathroom floor tiles must be R10 minimum. Shower area R11. Do not use smooth polished tiles on any bathroom floor.
  • Bathroom floor slope: The floor must slope toward the drain at 1 cm per meter. We will test with water after tiling and before grouting — water must flow to drain immediately. If any area pools water, you must redo it.
  • Staircase nosing: Every step of the staircase must have an anti-skid strip on the front edge of the step (step nosing). No smooth edges on any stair step. The step edge must be rounded or nosing strip applied — not sharp.
  • All wall corners throughout house: Use bullnose corner bead (rounded metal strip) on every internal corner during plastering. This is mandatory — no sharp 90-degree wall corners anywhere in the house.
  • Adhesive: Use the correct tile adhesive for each tile type. Do not substitute with plain cement+sand mortar for large format tiles.
  • Tap test after laying: After tiles are laid (before grouting), tap each tile with a hollow wooden block. A hollow sound means the tile is not properly bonded — those tiles must be relaid.
  • Grout: Grout colour to be confirmed with us before application. Use grout sealer on bathroom and kitchen grout after application.
  • Window sill tiles: Not to be used for external window sills (cracks due to expansion). Sill to be in granite or Kota stone — tiles only for walls and floors.
