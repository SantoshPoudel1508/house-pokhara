Hire First — Before Any Digging

Soil Test / Geotechnical Engineer

Tests what is underground before we design the foundation

What We Require You to Do
  • Minimum 2 boreholes on our 30×60 ft plot — front and rear
  • Drilling depth: minimum 25 feet (8 metres), or deeper if soft soil encountered
  • SPT (Standard Penetration Test) at every 1.5m of depth
  • Groundwater table level determination
  • We will be physically present during all drilling — please inform us in advance
  • We will take photographs and video of the drilling process
Report Must Include
  • Our specific plot address on the report — no address = not accepted
  • SBC (Safe Bearing Capacity) value clearly stated in kN/m²
  • Borehole log with depth measurements and soil type at each level
  • Groundwater level
  • Recommended foundation type for a G+3-capable design
  • Seismic site classification
  • Settlement risk assessment
  • Report signed by a licensed engineer on company letterhead
⚠️ We Will NOT Accept: A report without our plot address. A report delivered within 24 hours of booking (not enough time for real testing). Any generic copied report.
Agreement & Sign-Off

Hire Third

Architect

Designs the layout, look, and feel of the house

House Style & Character

"Modern Warm Minimal Family House" — warm materials, clean layout, natural light, peaceful atmosphere. Not trendy or flashy. Timeless and premium.

Non-Negotiable Design Requirements
  • Cross-ventilation: Every single room must have openings on at least two walls
  • Service zones: All bathrooms on every floor must be stacked directly above each other. Kitchen also aligned vertically.
  • Entrance mudroom: Covered porch (min 1.2m × 1.8m) with bench and shoe cabinet before main door
  • No sharp edges: All wall corners to have bullnose rounded bead throughout the entire house
  • Ground floor: Elderly-friendly — wide doors (1000mm+), no level differences, anti-skid floors, grab bar provision
  • Second floor: Must be designed to function independently (future rental) — own kitchen, bathroom, and separate staircase access
  • Music/hobby room: One room on first floor with thicker walls (230mm) and extra electrical conduit provisions
  • Setbacks: Front 8–10ft · Sides 3–4ft each · Rear 4–6ft (sewage side)
  • Window sills: Granite or Kota stone — not tiles — sloped outward with drip edge
  • Dust-proof storage room with sealed door and epoxy floor

After Structure, Before Tiling

Waterproofing Contractor

Terrace, bathrooms, rear wall — 48-hour flood test required

Terrace Waterproofing
  • Show us the sealed product tin before opening — we verify brand (Dr. Fixit, Fosroc, or equivalent)
  • Minimum 2 coats of polymer membrane — first coat fully cured before second
  • Area: entire terrace slab + all parapet walls up to top
  • 48-hour flood test mandatory — fill terrace with 50mm water, check ceiling below for dampness
  • No payment released and no terrace tiles laid until flood test passes and we sign off
Bathroom Waterproofing
  • 2 coats on all bathroom floors and walls up to 600mm height
  • Shower area: walls waterproofed to full shower height
  • 24-hour water test before any bathroom tile laying begins
Other Areas
  • Rear wall (sewage-side): extra 2-coat membrane + drainage slope away from house
  • All window and door frames: silicone sealant bead at junction — inside and outside

Finishing Phase

Tile Layer / Mason

Flooring, bathroom slope test, no sharp corners

Our Requirements
  • Check batch number on ALL tile boxes before laying — all must match, no mixing batches
  • Bathroom floor tiles: minimum R10 anti-skid rating — check specification sheet
  • Shower area: R11 anti-skid rating
  • Bathroom floor slope test: after tiling, pour water anywhere — must flow to drain within 10 seconds. Redo any area that pools water.
  • Staircase steps: anti-skid nosing strip on every step edge — rounded, not sharp
  • All wall corners throughout house: bullnose corner bead during plastering — no 90-degree sharp corners
  • Tap test: after laying, tap each tile — hollow sound = not bonded = relay
  • Window sills: granite or Kota stone only — not ceramic tiles

Interiors Phase

Modular Kitchen & Carpenter

Premium soft-close kitchen, rounded edges, suit-height wardrobes

Kitchen Hardware — Specific Brands Required
  • Drawer system: Blum Legrabox or Tandembox Antaro with Blumotion soft-close — OR — Hettich InnoTech Atira with SilentSystem. Show brand label on hardware.
  • Door hinges: Blum Clip Top Blumotion soft-close — OR — Hettich Sensys. No generic or local brand alternatives.
  • Cabinet carcass: 18mm BWR (Boiling Water Resistant) plywood, Grade 1 ISI marked. NOT particle board or MDF for carcass.
  • Countertop: Quartz stone (engineered) minimum 20mm thick. OR granite minimum 20mm. NOT laminate countertop.
No Sharp Edges — Mandatory Throughout
  • Countertop: eased edge or pencil round profile on every edge — no sharp 90-degree countertop edges
  • All cabinet doors and drawers: 2mm radius PVC edge banding — rounded profile
  • Any protruding cabinet corner at head/elbow height: rounded aluminium corner profile
Wardrobe Requirements
  • Full-length suit hanging section: rail at 185–190 cm from floor (we are 6 feet 1 inch tall — suits must hang without touching floor)
  • Total wardrobe height: 240 cm — floor to ceiling
  • Same soft-close hinges on all wardrobe doors
  • LED strip inside wardrobes on door-activated switch
