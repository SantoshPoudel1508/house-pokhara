Three Special Features You Asked For

1. Per-Room Master Switch (Outside Every Door)A single switch outside each bedroom door that turns off all lights inside that room. Press it when you leave — all lights off. This is done using a "relay module" or "master control switch" that your electrician installs per room. Ask your electrician specifically for this system.
2. Whole-House Master Switch (At Entrance)One switch at the main entrance that turns off ALL lights and fans in the entire house. BUT — fridge, CCTV, internet router, and security system must stay ON always. These must be on a separate "always-on" circuit that bypasses the master switch. Your electrician will design two circuit groups: one through master switch, one always-on bypass.
3. Separate Meter for 2nd Floor (G+2)The entire second floor must have its own electricity meter and its own distribution board from day one. All second-floor circuits run from this separate panel. This way, future tenants pay their own electricity bill separately.

Smart Electrical — Safety and Future-Proofing

Critical electrical details for Nepal conditions

Extra Tips
▼
⚠️ ImportantLightning protection is critical in Pokhara — the city has high lightning frequency due to proximity to hills. Install a lightning arrester (lightning rod) on the roof connected to a proper earthing system. One lightning strike can destroy all electronics in the house and start a fire.
✅ Do ThisLabel every single MCB (circuit breaker) in your distribution board with a permanent marker or label. Write what each one controls: "Bedroom 1 lights", "Kitchen sockets", "AC Bedroom 2". This seems minor but saves enormous confusion during repairs and when power cuts happen.
💡 Smart TipPokhara has frequent load shedding and voltage fluctuations. Install a Voltage Stabilizer on the main panel and/or individual stabilizers for sensitive equipment (refrigerator, TV, computer). Voltage fluctuations silently kill expensive appliances.
⚠️ ImportantInstall a surge protector device (SPD) in your main distribution board. During monsoon storms, power surges come through the electricity supply and fry all electronics. An SPD costs little but protects everything.
✅ Do ThisPlan for battery backup (inverter/UPS) from day one. Route a separate circuit for essential lights and fan in each bedroom through the inverter output. When power cuts happen at night, these rooms still have light without any switching needed.
💡 Smart TipEvery bathroom geyser switch MUST be outside the bathroom. This is both a safety requirement and a practical one — you switch on the geyser 20 minutes before entering the bathroom, not while standing wet inside.
