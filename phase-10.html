What Is a Grab Bar?

A grab bar is a strong fixed handle mounted on the bathroom wall.

Imagine a horizontal metal bar (like a bus or train handle) firmly screwed into the bathroom wall — at a height where you can hold it while sitting on the toilet or standing in the shower.

Why you need it:
• Your parents need it now for stability when sitting and standing from the toilet
• Anyone who is sick, tired, or has an injury uses it for support
• You will need it when you are older
• Bathroom falls are the most common cause of serious household injury in elderly people

Where to install (at minimum in one bathroom — ideally all):
• Beside the toilet — one bar on each side, at 75–80 cm height from floor
• Inside the shower — vertical bar at 90–120 cm height

Critical: The grab bar MUST be screwed into the wall structure with special anchors — or ideally into a small steel plate embedded in the wall during construction. A bar fixed only into tile with normal plastic plugs will pull out when someone's full body weight is applied — which is exactly when it is most needed. Tell your engineer: embed a small steel plate behind the bathroom wall at grab bar locations during construction.

Smart Bathrooms — Long-Term Comfort

Details that make bathrooms work perfectly for 20 years

Extra Tips
▼
💡 Smart TipGeyser size guide: 15 litre geyser = only enough for one quick shower. 25 litre = sufficient for one full shower with washing. 35 litre = two people can shower back to back. For your parents (ground floor), install 35 litre. For other bathrooms, 25 litre is fine.
⚠️ ImportantGeyser installation height: mount it as high as possible on the wall (close to ceiling). Hot water pressure is better when the geyser is higher than the shower. If the geyser is low, shower pressure becomes weak — especially noticeable when the tank is partially heated.
✅ Do ThisInstall a pressure relief valve (PRV) on every geyser. This is the small valve that releases pressure if the geyser overheats. Without PRV, a malfunctioning geyser can burst. This is mandatory safety equipment — check your electrician installs it.
💡 Smart TipMirror positioning: mount bathroom mirror so that the CENTER of the mirror is at average eye height of your family (typically 155–165cm from floor for most Nepali families). A mirror mounted too high or too low is uncomfortable daily.
⚠️ ImportantExhaust fan must discharge OUTSIDE the house — not into the ceiling void (between ceiling and slab). Exhausting into the ceiling void creates moisture, mould, and breeding ground for insects. Run the exhaust fan duct directly through the wall or roof to outside air.
✅ Do ThisFor elderly bathroom (parents room): install a fold-down shower seat or provision for one. This allows showering while seated which becomes necessary after any health issue, surgery, or in old age. The wall provision (embedded mounting plates) costs nothing now; the seat can be added later.

💡 Know This Recommendation for Pokhara: storage geyser (25–35 litre) in each bathroom. Simple, reliable, handles intermittent water pressure well. Instant geyser works poorly when water pressure is low — which is common in Nepal.

Hard Water Impact on Bathroom Fittings

⚠️ Critical Hard water (high mineral content) destroys bathroom fittings faster than you think. Scale deposits block tap aerators within 1 year, clog showerheads within 2 years, and build up inside geyser tanks reducing capacity by 30% within 5 years.
✅ Do This Solution: install tap aerators that can be unscrewed and cleaned (not fixed type). Clean aerators and showerheads monthly by soaking in white vinegar for 30 minutes — removes scale completely. Use descaler tablet in geyser tank once a year. If water is very hard (above 300 ppm), install a water softener on the main supply (Phase 4 covers this).

Bathroom Door — Open Outward or Inward?

💡 Know This Unlike other doors in the house (which should open inward), bathroom doors should open OUTWARD. Reason: if someone falls and is lying against the inward-opening door, rescuers cannot open it. An outward-opening bathroom door can always be opened from outside regardless of what is behind it on the inside.
✅ Do This Specify to your carpenter and door supplier: "All bathroom doors must open outward (into the corridor or bedroom, not into the bathroom)." This is especially important for the elderly parents' bathroom on the ground floor.
