What Is a Grab Bar?

A grab bar is a strong fixed handle mounted on the bathroom wall.

Imagine a horizontal metal bar (like a bus or train handle) firmly screwed into the bathroom wall — at a height where you can hold it while sitting on the toilet or standing in the shower.

Why you need it:
• Your parents need it now for stability when sitting and standing from the toilet
• Anyone who is sick, tired, or has an injury uses it for support
• You will need it when you are older
• Bathroom falls are the most common cause of serious household injury in elderly people

Where to install (at minimum in one bathroom — ideally all):
• Beside the toilet — one bar on each side, at 75–80 cm height from floor
• Inside the shower — vertical bar at 90–120 cm height

Critical: The grab bar MUST be screwed into the wall structure with special anchors — or ideally into a small steel plate embedded in the wall during construction. A bar fixed only into tile with normal plastic plugs will pull out when someone's full body weight is applied — which is exactly when it is most needed. Tell your engineer: embed a small steel plate behind the bathroom wall at grab bar locations during construction.
