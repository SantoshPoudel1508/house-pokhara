Shoe Removal Entrance Area — Called a Mudroom

What You Are Describing is called a Mudroom or Entrance Vestibule. This is a small transition space where outdoor shoes are removed before entering the main house. Excellent hygiene practice — outside dust and dirt stays at the door.
ElementDesignSize
Covered porchSmall covered area (with roof) outside main door — for removing shoes in any weatherMinimum 1.2m × 1.8m
Bench for sittingBuilt-in stone or wood bench — for sitting while removing shoesHeight 40–45 cm. Length 900mm minimum.
Shoe cabinetBuilt-in closed cabinet beside bench — closed doors hide shoes from guests. Ventilated back to prevent smell.3–4 tiers, space for 20+ pairs
Hooks for bags and coatsRow of sturdy hooks at 150–165cm height on wallMinimum 4 hooks. Stainless steel or cast iron — bags are heavy.
Floor materialAnti-skid natural stone (Kota stone) or rough ceramic R11 tile — visually different from inside house. This signals: remove shoes here.Match door width minimum
Optional step upSmall 50mm step up when entering the house — clearly marks the "clean inside" boundary. Common in Japanese homes.If doing this: round the step edge and add anti-skid strip
Indoor slipper rackSmall low open shelf just inside the door — for indoor slippers. One pair per family member + guest slippers.0–35 cm height, near entrance wall
Motion sensor lightAutomatic light in porch — comes on when someone arrives, especially at nightWaterproof outdoor LED

Rear Passage — Vegetable Garden Plan

Important: Confirm with your local municipality (Nagarpalika) that informal use of the public drainage passage for personal gardening is acceptable in your area. In most parts of Pokhara, this is informally tolerated for non-structural uses like vegetable plants.
OptionWhat to GrowNotes
Raised bed gardenTomatoes, chillies, coriander, methi, spinach, mintRaised brick or wood bed 30–40cm high — keeps roots out of drainage channel, easy to manage
Climbing plants on rear wallBeans, bitter gourd, ridge gourdGrow upward on wire trellis fixed to rear wall — uses vertical space
Pots in passageAny vegetables in large potsMost flexible — can be moved, no permanent construction in passage
  • Do NOT plant any tree with deep or large roots near the sewage passage or house foundation — roots damage pipes over years. Stick to vegetables and herbs only.
  • Plan one outdoor water tap at rear of house for garden watering
  • Rear wall must have good waterproofing before any soil is placed against it
  • Keep rear area well-drained — stagnant water breeds mosquitoes
