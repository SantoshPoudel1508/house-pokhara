Scam Guard — Read Before Paying Anyone

Structural, materials, plumbing, electrical, contract, and interior scams.
← ← Phase 12 Questions Guide → →
⚠️

All Major Scams With Exact Prevention Steps

12 scams common in Nepal/South Asia construction. Know these before paying anyone.

Must Read
▼
⚠️ Soil Test Without Real Drilling

Tester comes, takes photos, gives you a report copied from another site. Your actual soil is never tested.

✅ Be present. Watch drilling machine work in person. Take video. Report must show YOUR plot address. If not — reject the report.

⚠️ Extra Water Added to Concrete

Workers add extra water to make mixing easier. This weakens concrete by 30–40%. Your M25 becomes as weak as M10.

✅ Be present during concrete mixing. Tell foreman: 'No extra water.' Use vibrator machine instead.

⚠️ Steel Grade Substitution

Fe 415 sold as Fe 500D. Both look identical. Lower grade is cheaper — gives contractor extra profit.

✅ Ask for Mill Test Certificate with every delivery. The grade is also marked (rolled) onto the bar surface — look for the marking.

⚠️ Wrong Concrete Grade

M15 used but M25 billed. Cannot tell by looking.

✅ Take concrete cube samples during each pour. Send to testing lab at 7 days and 28 days. Ask structural engineer to arrange this.

⚠️ Skipping Curing

No water curing after pour. Concrete looks hard but is 30–40% weaker than designed.

✅ Check EVERY day that curing (wet cloth or water ponding) is being done for minimum 7 days on every structural element.

⚠️ Thin Wire Substitution

1.5 sq mm wire installed where 2.5 sq mm was quoted. Wire gauge is printed on the wire jacket.

✅ Check the wire spool being used each morning. Read the number printed on the outer jacket of the wire.

⚠️ Cheap Pipe Inside Walls

Branded pipe shown, unknown cheap brand installed inside walls where you cannot see.

✅ Check all pipe packaging before installation starts. Keep 2 cut sample pieces from each brand batch.

⚠️ One Waterproofing Coat Instead of Two

Two coats quoted, one coat applied. First monsoon shows roof leakage.

✅ Watch both coats applied in person. Count empty product containers against area treated.

⚠️ Particle Board Sold as Plywood

Kitchen cabinets made from cheap particle board but invoiced as plywood.

✅ Ask for a cut cross-section sample. Marine plywood shows alternating wood grain layers. Particle board shows compressed wood chip particles.

⚠️ Full Payment in Advance

Contractor takes 70–80% advance, then slows work, reduces quality, or disappears.

✅ Maximum 20–25% advance. All remaining payments by verified milestone. Written contract only.

⚠️ Tile Batch Mismatch

Half your floor looks slightly different shade — different production batches.

✅ Check batch number printed on EVERY tile box before buying. All boxes must show the same batch number.

⚠️ Paint Diluted With Extra Water

Painter adds extra water. Paint becomes thin and fades in 2 years.

✅ Mix paint in your presence. Good emulsion coats a finger clearly — very watery means too diluted.

🚨 Walk Away Immediately If You See Any of These:
  • Any contractor who says soil test is a waste of money
  • Anyone who offers a price far below market rate (cutting corners somewhere)
  • Contractor who cannot show you real built houses you can visit and inspect
  • Anyone who refuses to put materials, grades, and scope in writing
  • Demand for more than 30% advance payment before work begins
  • Contractor or engineer without a verifiable licence or registration number
  • Anyone who says "trust me, I've done hundreds of houses" to avoid documentation
