Columns, Beams, Walls — Building the Skeleton

Steel and concrete frame. Most important for earthquake safety.

Read Carefully
▼
No Sharp Edges on Wall Corners — Mandatory EverywhereAll columns and wall corners must have their sharp edges rounded during plastering. This is called a "bullnose corner." Ask your mason: "Use bullnose corner bead on every single internal corner." Cost: almost zero. Benefit: if a child or adult bumps into the corner, they do not get a sharp cut.

Dust Control During Construction

Construction Creates Enormous DustCutting bricks, mixing cement, grinding — all produce fine dust that settles everywhere and is very hard to clean from finished surfaces.
✅ Insist contractor uses wet cutting methods (blade with water) for tiles and blocks
✅ Cover all finished surfaces and windows with plastic sheets while work continues nearby
✅ After all construction, do a full wet cleaning before painting starts
✅ Window and door frame channels must be covered with tape during rough work to prevent dust jamming them
