Soft-Close Kitchen — What It Is and Which Brands to Ask For

The smooth slow-closing drawers you see in Apple office kitchens are called "Soft-Close" or "Self-Close" drawers.

A small hydraulic damper inside the drawer channel slows the drawer down automatically when it is almost closed (last 40mm). No matter how hard you push, it closes gently. Same with cabinet doors — the hinge has a damper.

Brands to ask for specifically:
• Blum (Austrian) — The gold standard. Drawer system: "Legrabox" or "Tandembox Antaro" with "Blumotion" soft-close. Door hinge: "Clip Top Blumotion." This is exactly what premium offices use worldwide.
• Hettich (German) — Slightly more affordable. Drawer system: "InnoTech Atira" with "SilentSystem." Door hinge: "Sensys" range.

Tell your kitchen supplier: "I want Blum Legrabox drawers and Blum Clip Top Blumotion hinges." They will know exactly what you mean. If they offer a local brand saying "same quality" — it is NOT the same.

Height-Adjustable Sit-Stand Table (Study / Office)

What It Is: A table whose height you can change — low for sitting, high for standing. Press a button and the table goes up or down electrically. Very good for back health and excellent for children studying.

For you (6 feet 1 inch tall):
• Sitting height: 73–78 cm from floor
• Standing height: 105–115 cm from floor (your elbow height when standing)
Make sure the table you buy reaches at least 115 cm in standing mode.

Brands to look for: FlexiSpot, Autonomous, or ask a furniture maker to install an electric lift frame with a custom tabletop made to your size.

Dust-Free Storage Room Design

How to Make It Truly Dust-Free:
1. Door: UPVC door (does not warp or gap) with rubber seal strip all around the frame — like a refrigerator seal
2. Floor: Epoxy coating — sealed, no dust from floor surface, easy to wipe
3. Walls and ceiling: Smooth paint finish (not textured). Light colour to see dust easily.
4. Ventilation: One small filtered vent near top of wall — air pressure equalization without letting dust in. Replace filter every 6–12 months.
5. Shelving: Metal wire shelving — dust does not settle as easily as on wood shelves
6. All gaps: Seal every pipe or conduit hole in walls with silicone
