Window Sill Guide — What Your Father Is Planning to Build

The window sill is the stone ledge outside each window that sticks out from the wall. Here is how it must be built:

Best Material: Granite or Kota Stone
• Granite: Very hard, does not crack, water-resistant, premium look, very long life. ✅ Recommended.
• Kota Stone: Cheaper, good durability, natural look. ✅ Also good.
• NOT ceramic tiles: Tiles expand and contract differently from concrete — they crack at the joint within 3–5 years.
• NOT plain plastered concrete without protection: Will crack and stain within 2 monsoons.

How the Sill Must Be Built:
1. Stone piece should be wider than the window frame by 30–50mm on each side (like a picture frame)
2. The outer edge must slope downward slightly — so rain drips off the front edge and does not run back along the wall below the window
3. On the underside of the stone: carve a small groove (called a "drip groove") so water drips off cleanly
4. At the junction where stone meets wall: apply waterproof silicone sealant all around — inside and outside
5. Stone must be bedded in waterproof mortar

Without this: every rain runs down the wall below the window, causing damp patches and paint damage for years.
