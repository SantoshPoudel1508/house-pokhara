📱 How to Add Documents — No uploads needed, completely free
  1. Open Google Drive on your phone or computer
  2. Upload or find your document (photo, PDF, scan, etc.)
  3. Tap the 3-dot menu on the file → "Share" → "Anyone with the link can view"
  4. Copy the link
  5. Come back here → click "Add Document" → paste the link
The document is stored safely in your Google Drive. This website just saves the link and shows it organised by category.
