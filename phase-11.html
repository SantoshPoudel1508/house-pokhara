No Sharp Edges — Every Location in the House

LocationSolutionWhen
All wall corners (entire house)"Bullnose corner bead" — a rounded metal strip on every internal corner during plastering. Creates ~12mm radius curve instead of sharp 90-degree corner.During plastering — cannot change after
Kitchen counter edgesEased or pencil round edge profile on all countertop edgesDuring countertop order
Cabinet door and drawer edgesRounded 2mm PVC edge banding on all exposed edgesDuring cabinet manufacturing
Staircase step front edgeRounded nosing profile + anti-skid strip on every step edgeDuring staircase construction
Staircase handrail endsSmooth return cap (curved end piece) at top and bottom of every handrail — prevents protruding end from poking eyes of childrenDuring staircase installation
Window sill corners5–10mm chamfer (angled cut) on all four corners of granite sill — quarry does this if askedDuring stone cutting
All furnitureSpecify "no sharp edges, radius corner" to every furniture supplier and carpenterWhen ordering
TV unit and shelvingAll shelf edges rounded — same PVC edge banding as kitchen cabinetsDuring carpentry work

Music and Hobby Room — Plan Now, Build Later

Smart Approach: The expensive part (soundproofing treatment) can be added later when you are ready. But some things are much cheaper to do NOW during construction.

Do NOW (during construction — very low extra cost):
1. Designate one room on the first floor for this purpose
2. Use thicker walls for that room: 230mm wall (two bricks thick) instead of standard 115mm — doubles sound insulation
3. Install conduit pipes in ceiling and walls for future audio cables and speaker wires
4. Plan 6 power sockets in this room and a dedicated AC point
5. Embed small steel plates in ceiling for future speaker mounting

Do LATER (when ready):
• Acoustic foam panels on walls
• Bass trap panels in corners
• Thick carpet on floor
• Heavy curtains on window
• Door with rubber gasket seal

FloorPurposeKey Design Rules
Ground FloorParents' bedroom, kitchen, living/dining, guest toilet, puja room, entrance mudroom, utility roomElderly-friendly: wide doors (1000mm), no level differences inside, anti-skid floors everywhere, grab bar in at least one bathroom, morning sunlight
First FloorYour master bedroom, children's rooms, family lounge, home office/study, music/hobby roomYour master bedroom gets morning sunlight (NE advantage). Office accessible without going through bedrooms. Music room has thicker walls.
Second FloorIndependent 2-bedroom flat for future rental or childrenCan function independently: separate entrance access, separate meter, own kitchen and bathroom
