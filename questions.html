Questions to Ask Every Professional

Print this and take it to every meeting

Complete List
▼
🔬 Soil Test Company
  • Will you physically drill with a machine? Can I be present?
  • What depth will you drill to and why?
  • Will I receive a signed report with my exact plot address on it?
  • Have you done soil tests in this specific area of Pokhara before?
🏛️ Structural Engineer
  • Are you registered with Nepal Engineering Council? What is your NEC number?
  • Will you design for G+3 even though we are building G+2?
  • What concrete grade for columns, beams, and slabs? What steel grade?
  • How many times will you visit the site during construction?
  • Will you check the steel before every single concrete pour?
📐 Architect
  • Can I see real photos of houses you have designed and built (not computer renders)?
  • Will every room have openings on two walls for cross-ventilation?
  • Will you plan service zones (all bathrooms stacked vertically)?
  • Will the design include a covered entrance shoe area (mudroom)?
  • Will the music/hobby room have thicker walls planned from the start?
👷 Contractor / Builder
  • Can I visit 2 previous houses you have built and speak to those homeowners?
  • Do you own a concrete vibrator machine? Can I see it?
  • Will you give me a written contract with all material grades listed?
  • What is your curing policy after concrete pours?
  • Will the structural engineer check steel before every concrete pour?
🔧 Plumber
  • Will you draw a complete pipe layout plan before starting any work?
  • Will all bathrooms be directly above each other on every floor (service zones)?
  • Are you using CPVC pipes for hot water lines (not regular uPVC)?
  • Will every drain have a P-trap installed?
  • How will you test pipes before the walls are plastered and closed?
⚡ Electrician
  • Can you draw a room-by-room electrical layout before running any wire?
  • Will each room have a master control switch outside the door?
  • Will the whole-house switch have an always-on bypass for fridge and CCTV?
  • Will the second floor have a completely separate electricity meter?
  • What wire brand and exactly what square mm for each type of circuit?
💦 Waterproofing Contractor
  • Which brand of product are you using? Show me the sealed tin before opening.
  • How many coats are you applying on the terrace?
  • Will you do a 48-hour flood test before terrace tiles are laid?
  • What warranty are you giving on your waterproofing work?
🔲 Tile Layer / Mason
  • Will you check that all tile boxes have the same batch number before laying?
  • Will you test bathroom floor slope with water before applying grout?
  • Will all wall corners throughout the house have bullnose rounded bead?
  • Will you do a tap test on every tile after laying to check for hollow spots?
🎨 Painter
  • How many coats of primer and paint are you applying?
  • Are you using exterior weatherproof paint on outdoor walls (not interior paint)?
  • Will you mix paint in my presence so I can check it is not too diluted?
  • Is the kitchen and bathroom paint semi-gloss (washable surface)?
🍳 Kitchen / Carpenter
  • What hardware brand for drawers and hinges? (Must be Blum or Hettich.)
  • What is the cabinet carcass material? (Must be 18mm BWR plywood.)
  • Will all edges — countertop, cabinet doors, shelves — be rounded (no sharp edges)?
  • Can I visit a previous kitchen you have installed?
🪟 Window Supplier
  • Is this UPVC profile with thermal break?
  • Does it include integrated retractable mosquito mesh?
  • What is the glass thickness and type?
  • What is the warranty on the profile, hardware, and glass?
🏪 Any Material Shop (Steel / Cement / Tiles)
  • Can you provide the quality certificate or test report for this material?
  • What is the manufacturing date or production date?
  • Can I test a sample or weigh a few pieces?
  • If this material is found below specification, what is your return policy?
